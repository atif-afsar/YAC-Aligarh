# Phase 4 — CRM, Unified Inbox & Human Handoff

## Objective
Create a central place for YAC staff to manage conversations and leads from every channel.

## Scope
- Unified inbox.
- Website/WhatsApp/Instagram filters.
- Conversation history.
- Lead profiles.
- Lead status.
- Lead scoring.
- Assignment to staff.
- Notes/tags.
- Human takeover.
- Search/filter.
- Conversation audit trail.

## Lead Fields
Name, phone, email, course, class, location, mode, source, score, status, assigned agent, timestamps, notes.

## Lead States
New → Contacted → Qualified → Hot/Warm/Cold → Counselling → Converted/Lost.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Every supported channel creates a unified conversation record.
- Leads can be searched and filtered.
- Staff can take over from AI.
- Staff can return conversation to AI according to permissions.
- Conversation and lead history remains available.
