# Phase 3 — Instagram AI Automation

## Objective
Connect YAC's eligible Instagram Professional account to the central AI agent.

## Scope
- Meta Developer App configuration.
- Instagram Professional account connection.
- Required permissions.
- Webhook/event handling.
- Incoming DM processing.
- AI response generation.
- Conversation persistence.
- Lead creation/update.
- Human handoff.
- Supported comment-to-conversation workflows where permitted.

## Flow
Instagram interaction → Meta webhook → backend → AI agent → knowledge/tools → response → Instagram.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Supported Instagram account is connected.
- Test DM reaches backend.
- AI responds with YAC-approved information.
- Conversation appears in admin inbox.
- Lead source is marked Instagram.
- Human takeover works.
